
# ComixRus – Business Problem

## 1. Business Background

ComixRus is an independent comic book publisher with an established and loyal customer base. The business publishes digital comic books and works with independent writers to produce new content.

The business currently operates a relatively small publishing website with limited technical resources.

## 2. Current Business Challenges

The existing platform is struggling to support the growth of the business.

Key challenges include:

- Poor website performance during periods of high customer demand.
- Inconsistent availability and website glitches.
- Limited ability to scale resources in response to fluctuating traffic.
- A need for a more structured writer submission and editorial review process.
- A requirement to support secure digital purchases and permanent customer access to purchased comics.

## 3. Business Objectives

The new platform should:

1. Improve website reliability and customer experience.
2. Support unpredictable increases in website traffic.
3. Allow writers to submit comic books digitally.
4. Provide an editorial approval process before publication.
5. Allow customers to browse comic samples without registering.
6. Support customer registration and secure authentication.
7. Enable purchases through Stripe and PayPal.
8. Provide customers with permanent access to purchased digital comics.

## 4. Proposed Outcome

ComixRus wants a modern, scalable and secure digital publishing platform that can support business growth without requiring a large internal technical team.

The solution should reduce operational overhead, improve customer satisfaction and provide a foundation for future expansion.

## 5. Project Scope

### In Scope
- Customer-facing publishing website.
- Writer submission portal.
- Editorial review and approval workflow.
- Customer registration and authentication.
- Digital comic catalogue and storage.
- Payment integration.
- Customer purchase history and digital library.
- Asynchronous notifications.

### Out of Scope
- Physical comic book production and distribution.
- Native mobile applications.
- Social networking functionality.
- Automated comic creation.
